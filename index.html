About Brezoid

Built on Integrity.
Driven by Results.

Founded in 2004, Brezoid Global Networks Limited has grown from a boutique Nairobi firm into one of East Africa's most respected mid-market advisory practices. We combine the rigour of the Big Four with the agility and personal attention of an independent firm.

Our 35-strong team of CPAs, tax specialists, and forensic accountants serves corporates, NGOs, SMEs, and high-net-worth individuals across Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, and Rwanda.

  • Full ICPAK membership in good standing since 2004
  • Registered KRA Tax Agent (TPA/2004/0142)
  • ISQM-1 quality management framework adopted firm-wide
  • ISO 27001-aligned data security for all client information
