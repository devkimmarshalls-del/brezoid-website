Warning Signs

When Should You
Call a Forensic Accountant?

Unexplained cash shortfalls or missing inventory

Regular reconciliation differences with no obvious cause.

Supplier invoices that don't match contracted terms

Prices above market rate, round-sum invoices, or sole-source suppliers.

An employee living significantly beyond their salary

Unexplained properties, vehicles, or lifestyle changes.

A financial dispute requiring independent quantification

Shareholder disputes, breach of contract, or insurance claims.

A whistleblower report or anonymous tip

Allegations require independent investigation — not internal review.

Donor or grant fund discrepancies

Unaccounted project expenditures or unexplained fund balances.

Investigation Methodology

Our Forensic
Investigation Process

01

Confidential Briefing & NDA

Initial briefing with board or legal counsel under NDA. We assess the situation, scope the investigation, and confirm whether we can accept the engagement without conflict.

02

Evidence Preservation

Immediate steps to preserve financial records, system access logs, and documentary evidence — maintaining full chain of custody for legal admissibility.

03

Financial Analysis & Testing

Deep-dive transaction analysis, Benford's Law testing, journal entry review, and document examination using forensic data analysis tools.

04

Interviews & Corroboration

Structured interviews with relevant parties conducted in coordination with legal counsel — findings independently corroborated against documentary evidence.

05

Forensic Report

Comprehensive investigation report with findings, methodology, evidence references, and conclusions — structured to court-admissible standards.

06

Remediation & Recovery Support

Post-investigation support for asset recovery proceedings, disciplinary processes, police reporting, and control remediation to prevent recurrence.
