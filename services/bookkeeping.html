How It Works

Our Monthly
Bookkeeping Cycle

A predictable, repeatable process so nothing ever slips through the cracks.

01

Data Collection (1st“3rd)

You share bank statements, invoices, and receipts via our secure portal or email. We send you a simple monthly checklist so nothing is missed.

02

Posting & Reconciliation (3rd“7th)

Your bookkeeper posts all transactions, reconciles every account, and flags any anomalies or missing documents for your attention.

03

Review & Query Resolution (7th“9th)

A senior reviewer checks all postings. Any queries are resolved with you directly ’ no assumptions, no guesswork.

04

Monthly Close & Reporting (by 10th)

Books closed, trial balance delivered, and your monthly summary report shared ’ ready for management account preparation or tax filing.

05

Archive & Audit Trail

All documents archived digitally with a full audit trail maintained ’ searchable, secure, and ready for any KRA or external audit request.
