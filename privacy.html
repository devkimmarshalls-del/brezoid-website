Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025  ·  Effective: June 2025

Brezoid Global Networks Limited ("Brezoid", "we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights under applicable Kenyan and international data protection law, including the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019.

1. Who We Are

Brezoid Global Networks Limited is a registered company in Kenya (KRA Tax Agent TPA/2004/0142), with offices at Ecobank Towers, Mama Ngina Street, Jubilee Exchange Building, 4th Floor, Nairobi. We are the data controller for personal information collected through this website and our service delivery.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of personal data:

  • Contact information — name, email address, phone number, company name
  • Enquiry information — service interest, message content submitted through contact or quote forms
  • Technical data — IP address, browser type, pages visited, time on site (via analytics)
  • Client data — financial, tax, and business information provided during service engagements (governed by separate engagement terms)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal data to:

  • Respond to enquiries and provide the services you request
  • Send KRA deadline alerts and tax updates (only where you have subscribed)
  • Improve our website and services through analytics
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations
  • Prevent fraud and ensure the security of our systems

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your data on the following legal bases:

  • Consent — where you have opted in to communications
  • Contract — where processing is necessary to deliver services you have engaged us for
  • Legitimate interests — for analytics and website improvement
  • Legal obligation — where required by Kenyan law

5. Cookies

This website uses cookies to improve your experience. Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We use:

  • Essential cookies — required for the website to function
  • Analytics cookies — to understand how visitors use our site (only with your consent)

You can manage cookie preferences through the cookie banner on your first visit, or by adjusting your browser settings at any time.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share it with:

  • Service providers who assist in operating our website and communications (e.g. email delivery, form processing)
  • Professional advisors under confidentiality obligations
  • Regulatory authorities where required by law

7. Data Retention

We retain personal data for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes outlined in this policy, or as required by law. Client engagement data is retained for a minimum of 7 years in accordance with Kenyan tax and audit regulations.

8. Your Rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Object to or restrict processing
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at operations@brezoidglobal.co.ke.

9. Data Security

We implement ISO 27001-aligned security controls including encryption, access controls, and annual penetration testing to protect your data against unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure.

10. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices and encourage you to review their policies.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of our website after changes constitutes acceptance.
